## Decisions to check

- **The card isn't live yet.** The first criterion asks for Play or Continue on `/it`. Those states are built and unit tested, but they sit behind the same `ready` flag the home page uses, which stays `false`. Opening it now would lead players to an empty screen, and the giallo can't ship before PLAY-082. Flipping that one flag opens it on `/` and `/it` together.
- **Chi è? keeps its existing layout.** Its Play button and levels stay as they were, with the giallo's card beside them, rather than becoming a card of their own. This keeps its Home e2e tests passing unedited.
- **`Solve a case` rather than `Play`.** GS 9.1 says Play, but two `Play` controls on one screen would be ambiguous, and Chi è?'s tests find their button by that name.
